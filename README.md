# OneClean

Downloads for [OneClean](https://oneclean-web.vercel.app/), a free Mac cleaner that shows you everything before anything is removed.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/Snehasish-Konger/oneclean-releases/releases/latest/download/OneClean.dmg)** · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple silicon and Intel

## Install

1. Open `OneClean.dmg` and drag OneClean into Applications.
2. Open OneClean from Applications. macOS will say it can't check the app, because OneClean isn't notarized by Apple yet. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to the OneClean message. Confirm with your password.
4. OneClean opens. You only need to do this once.

OneClean is free and isn't yet registered with Apple's paid developer programme, which is why macOS asks. The app never deletes anything without asking you first.
